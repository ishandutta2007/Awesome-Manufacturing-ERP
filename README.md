# Awesome-Manufacturing-ERP

## Top Manufacturing ERP Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Production Planning, MRP, BOM Management, Shop-Floor Control, Inventory & Discrete/Process Manufacturing*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Manufacturing ERP**. These systems integrate financials, inventory, production planning (MRP), bill of materials, work orders, quality, and shop-floor execution to run manufacturing operations end-to-end.



**Examples** include Infor CloudSuite Industrial, SYSPRO, Epicor Kinetic, IFS Cloud, Oracle NetSuite Manufacturing, SAP S/4HANA Manufacturing, Acumatica Manufacturing, Odoo Manufacturing, MRPeasy, and Katana MRP (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Manufacturing ERP has solid open-source options. **Odoo** (with its Manufacturing/MRP apps) and **ERPNext** are the most widely adopted self-hostable solutions with real production capabilities. Additional frameworks and lighter MRP tools exist for smaller operations. This section is heavily expanded.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Infor CloudSuite Industrial](https://www.infor.com/)**  

  Cloud ERP suite tailored for industrial and discrete manufacturers with deep production, inventory, and supply-chain capabilities.



- **[SYSPRO](https://www.syspro.com/)**  

  Manufacturing-focused ERP strong in discrete, process, and mixed-mode environments with robust inventory and quality management.



- **[Epicor Kinetic](https://www.epicor.com/)**  

  Cloud ERP designed for manufacturers, offering industry-specific depth in production planning, shop-floor, and distribution.



- **[IFS Cloud](https://www.ifs.com/)**  

  Enterprise cloud ERP particularly strong for complex manufacturing, asset-intensive, and project-based industries (aerospace, industrial equipment, etc.).



- **[Oracle NetSuite Manufacturing](https://www.netsuite.com/)**  

  Cloud ERP manufacturing module within NetSuite covering BOM, work orders, MRP, and shop-floor control for mid-sized manufacturers.



- **[SAP S/4HANA Manufacturing](https://www.sap.com/)**  

  Flagship ERP manufacturing capabilities from SAP for discrete and process industries, with advanced planning and execution features.



- **[Acumatica Manufacturing](https://www.acumatica.com/)**  

  Flexible cloud ERP with manufacturing editions supporting production management, MRP, and inventory for growing manufacturers.



- **[Odoo Manufacturing (Enterprise / Odoo.sh)](https://www.odoo.com/)**  

  Commercial and hosted offerings of Odoo’s manufacturing suite (MRP, work centers, quality, maintenance) on top of the open-source core.



- **[MRPeasy](https://www.mrpeasy.com/)**  

  Cloud MRP and production planning software aimed at small to mid-sized manufacturers seeking simplicity and speed.



- **[Katana MRP](https://katanamrp.com/)**  

  Modern cloud manufacturing and inventory platform popular with product-based and make-to-order businesses.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Odoo (Community) Manufacturing](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Leading open-source ERP with mature Manufacturing, MRP, BOM, work order, quality, and maintenance modules used by many manufacturers worldwide.



- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Fully open-source ERP (Python/Frappe) with comprehensive manufacturing support including BOM, work orders, capacity planning, subcontracting, and shop-floor tracking.



- **[Dolibarr](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)**  

  Lightweight open-source ERP/CRM with basic manufacturing and production modules suitable for smaller operations.



- **[Tryton](https://github.com/tryton/tryton)**  

  Modular open-source business application platform with production and inventory components that can be assembled into a manufacturing system.



- **[Metasfresh](https://github.com/metasfresh/metasfresh)**  

  Open-source ERP focused on distribution and manufacturing with MRP and production capabilities.



- **[Axelor](https://github.com/axelor/axelor-open-suite)**  

  Open-source business suite including production and manufacturing process management with strong BPM features.



- **[Opseron and emerging open enterprise platforms](https://github.com/)**  

  Newer open-source platforms aiming to cover ERP + MRP + project and operations workflows in a unified codebase.



- **[Open BOM and product-structure tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Community libraries and applications for managing bills of materials and product configurations.



- **[Shop-floor data collection open prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight open tools for work-order tracking, time booking, and simple MES-style data capture.



- **[Inventory and warehouse open modules](https://github.com/)**  

  Open inventory management components frequently combined with Odoo or ERPNext for manufacturing environments.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Starting with **Odoo Community** when a broad ERP + strong manufacturing app ecosystem is needed.

- Choosing **ERPNext** for a fully open, Python-based system with solid MRP and production features out of the box.

- Evaluating lighter options (Dolibarr, Tryton, Metasfresh) for smaller manufacturers or specific process needs.

- Combining open ERP cores with open MES or quality tools for a more complete manufacturing stack.

- Accepting that deep industry-specific functionality, advanced APS, global multi-plant support, and long-term vendor SLAs still favor commercial platforms (Infor, SYSPRO, Epicor, IFS, SAP, NetSuite, Acumatica, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, customization, and cost control for SMBs and mid-market manufacturers.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Implement core manufacturing processes (BOM, MRP, work orders, inventory) in Odoo or ERPNext → extend with quality and maintenance modules → integrate shop-floor devices or simple MES → connect accounting and purchasing. Suitable for manufacturers with internal IT or implementation partners. Many larger and highly regulated manufacturers continue to standardize on commercial manufacturing ERP suites.
